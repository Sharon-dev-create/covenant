# Financial model

M2-D local implementation uses the immutable manager's fixed 5% APR, bound to the signed manager address. For principal `P`, elapsed seconds `t`, and reduced carry `c`, compute `n = P*t+c`, whole increment `floor(n/630720000)` and carry `n mod 630720000`. The implementation decomposes the product to avoid intermediate overflow when the result fits. Preview reports whole interest separately and adds one unit to the total payoff for nonzero carry. A partial repayment that extinguishes principal one unit short of payoff converts that carry to one unit of outstanding interest. No further interest accrues after principal reaches zero. Full payoff and all transfers occur atomically.

Example: 200 USDC outstanding for one year accrues 10 USDC. Paying 5 credits the lender 5, leaves principal 200 and interest 5. Paying another 55 applies 5 to interest and 50 to principal, leaving principal 150. Cash grows by 60 in total; the remaining 150 is a receivable.

Version 2 local agreement authorization binds independent borrower and lender nonces. Each signer can invalidate an unused nonce in its own role without changing registered agreements or reservations. Both nonces are consumed atomically at registration. See the [signing vector](M2_SIGNING_VECTOR.md).

Use integer wei and six-decimal USDC units. Normalize both feeds to a common precision. With positive ETH/USD and USDC/USD prices, compute `value = floor(wei * ethPrice * 10^6 / (10^18 * usdcPrice))` using overflow-safe full-precision math and conservative rounding. Validate round, price bounds, timestamp, configured heartbeat/freshness and sequencer availability/grace period where supported. Missing usable feeds block real-value origination. For the harness only, use fixed USDC/USD of 1 and controllable ETH/USD. A Base ETH/USD view is an economic proxy for Unichain ETH, subject to chain/custody risk and redemption basis.

Initial illustrative parameters are 50% origination/draw LTV, 5% simple annual interest, zero commitment fee, one draw, and no automatic cross-chain liquidation. Set draw deadline and maturity separately, maturity later. Signed cap must fit current LTV at activation. At draw compute headroom from current valuation and subtract existing interest plus principal (zero before the first draw). Spend at most the minimum of headroom, undrawn contractual cap, reservation, market debt and purpose policy cap. Enforce minimum useful repayment and post-call risk improvement. USDC cash and debt are independent.

For each lender cash buckets are free and reserved. Deposit increases free. Reserve moves free to reserved. Uncommit moves reserved to free. Only the adapter's net verified spend reduces vault cash and creates equal principal; with one draw, return unused commitment to free immediately. Withdrawals are bounded by lender free cash. Repayment increases free only by actual receipt. Interest grows only on outstanding principal. Accrue `principal * aprBps * elapsed / (10000 * 365 days)` using a carry remainder in constant-denominator units so checkpoint frequency does not alter accrual. Apply floor to intermediate interest, ceil the final carried fraction by at most one unit when principal is paid off, and clear carry. Roll the accrual checkpoint back with failed transfers. Interest continues through default until a later settlement mechanism explicitly freezes a claim. Zero commitment fees in M1 avoid a separate debt/release accounting path and leave reservation opportunity cost with the lender. Future fees should accrue on unused committed inventory only over the active commitment window, separately from principal, with explicit funding and release terms.

Anyone may cure using USDC; credit the lender, apply interest then principal, and pull at most the debt owed. Full repayment closes after accounting; partial repayment leaves the remainder open. Draw expiry cancels unused reservations. Maturity plus agreed grace permits permissionless default when debt remains; repayment is allowed while defaulted until an authorized liquidation transition takes over. Price-based Covenant default/liquidation is deferred. Cancellation is permitted before a draw, including for the borrower relinquishing unused active credit. The lender cannot revoke an active commitment. Closing cancels pending execution on Base atomically and produces a terminal release only with zero claim.

## Recovery design gate

Proposed future recovery uses a prefunded Base settlement provider that escrows Circle USDC against a named default and posts a fixed quote specifying amount, ETH recipient, expiry, max fees and minimum proceeds. After finalized Base escrow and an irreversible liquidation award, Unichain can assign a claim on locked ETH to the provider. The Base escrow converts to lender proceeds only on independently verified finalized Unichain assignment under the matching settlement ID. To avoid a provider refund racing a delayed assignment, refund requires finalized Unichain cancellation proving assignment is now impossible. Once assigned, the provider bears bridge/swap/inventory risk. This route requires real provider liquidity, quote/auction rules, and verified two-way finality and dispute mechanics. An alternative sale of ETH for Unichain Circle USDC and CCTP transfer requires real execution liquidity, a verified route and attributable Base receipt; messaging alone moves no value.

Apply actual Base proceeds against the frozen claim and explicitly record principal and interest shortfalls as lender losses only once recovery is final and the approved policy permits write-off. Return surplus to the borrower after fees and lender claim, with separately accounted balances. Unresolved or delayed settlement remains open. M1 default freezes release and permits cure; liquidation, write-off and bridge settlement stay disabled.

## M1-A accounting

The proposed M2 manager acts as both immutable vault manager and consumer in a new deployment. It draws an exact precomputed amount and reverts the entire transaction unless the approved market applies that amount. A new manager-only `creditRepayment(reservationId,paymentId,payer,amount)` vault entry point derives the lender from the consumed reservation and credits only an exact in-call token receipt. See [M2 integration design](M2_CREDIT_ENGINE.md) for the complete atomic flow, payer authorization and numeric example. This is a proposal and does not change deployed contracts.

For total vault cash `B`, free `F`, reserved `R` and unsolicited surplus `S`, maintain `B = F + R + S`. A draw `d` from reservation `r` has `B' = B-d`, `R' = R-r`, `F' = F+r-d` and principal `P' = P+d` only after verified repayment. A measured borrower payment `x` has `B' = B+x`, `F' = F+x`, `R' = R`, `S' = S`; apply `min(x,I)` to interest and the remainder to principal. A payment above the current claim reverts before pulling tokens. Outstanding receivables are excluded from vault cash.

`managedCash = totalFree + totalReserved`. The measured vault balance also includes `surplus = tokenBalance - managedCash`. Surplus confers no withdrawal rights. Deposits require exact sender debit and exact vault credit. Withdrawals and consumption require exact vault debit and exact recipient credit. Fee-on-transfer tokens and elastic supplies are outside the supported asset assumptions. An unused consent cap is revocable, while a reservation removes cash from the lender's free balance.

A successful local consumption closes the reservation in one transaction, reduces vault cash by the exact transfer, and returns any unused reserve to free. It tests cash movement with a fixture consumer. M1-A records no receivables, interest or LTV enforcement. A future manager must book principal only after a successful purpose-bound repayment. Outstanding receivables are amounts owed by borrowers. They sit outside free cash, reserved cash and the balance available for withdrawal or reservation. Only measured repayment received by the vault can increase lender cash. The proposed 50% maximum initial LTV, 5% simple annual rate, zero commitment fee and one non-revolving draw remain the target model.

## Enforced M2 financial operating envelope

The local manager now enforces, in bytecode and before an agreement can exist:

| Limit | Enforced value | Enforcement point |
|---|---:|---|
| Commitment (`terms.cap`) | 1,000,000 USDC = 1,000,000,000,000 base units | `register`, before nonce consumption or reservation |
| Principal / exact draw (`terms.autonomousRepayment`) | 1,000,000 USDC = 1,000,000,000,000 base units | `register`, rechecked in `_validateDraw` before principal creation |
| Asset | six-decimal USDC-compatible token | manager constructor checks `decimals() == 6`; exact balance deltas remain mandatory |
| APR | fixed simple 5%, `APR_BPS = 500` | immutable constant; no per-agreement rate field |
| Contractual term | `maturity - drawDeadline <= 365 days` | `register`; grace is post-maturity and does not extend the contractual term |
| Timestamp representation | uint64 | all recorded draw/accrual timestamps and all deadline comparisons use checked uint64 conversion |

Interest accrues from the draw timestamp on outstanding principal only, continues after maturity and after `markDefault`, and remains repayable until the debt is actually paid. The contractual 365-day term is **not** an interest cutoff. The supported interest accrual period is the full uint64 timestamp range (`MAX_ACCRUAL_SECONDS = 2^64 - 1`), because both the checkpoint and the draw timestamp are checked uint64 values. No interest saturation, debt forgiveness, silent truncation or arbitrary cutoff was introduced.

### Arithmetic bounds

Let `P <= 1e12`, `T = 2^64 - 1`, `R < D` and `D = 630720000 = 10000 * 365 days / 500`. For elapsed seconds `t <= T`, the checkpoint increment is `floor((P*t + R)/D)` with remainder `(P*t + R) mod D`. The largest tail product is `(D-1)^2 < 3.98e17`; the largest quotient products are below `2^76`. Because the carry recurrence telescopes, cumulative recognized interest across any number of checkpoints equals `floor(P*(now - draw)/D)` from a zero initial carry, bounded by `MAX_SUPPORTED_INTEREST = 29247120867753601621956` base units. The full claim, including the single final rounding unit, is at most `MAX_SUPPORTED_CLAIM = 29247120868753601621956`, roughly `2.5e-56` of `2^256 - 1`. Principal paid is bounded by the original principal; interest paid and cumulative accrued interest are bounded by the interest bound. Vault aggregates use checked uint256 arithmetic and exact token receipts, so no supported lifecycle value can wrap silently.

The previous model permitted a signed and funded extreme cap. With `P = floor((2^256 - 1)/2)` and `t = D`, the single-word claim reached `2^256 - 2`; one second later `debtPreview` and every repayment reverted, making an accepted obligation unpayable. The new envelope rejects that agreement at signature submission, before any state or liquidity is committed, while preserving normal-value behavior.

## M3-B testnet policy

The same abstract financial core backs the 31337 local manager and 84532 testnet wrapper. The testnet wrapper caps both per-agreement commitment and principal at 300e6 six-decimal units and successful lifetime draw principal at 300e6 per manager. The lifetime amount is not cash and is never reduced by repayment, default or cancellation. A failed draw—including one above remaining lifetime capacity—reverts all market/vault effects and does not consume capacity. The M2 interest formula, carry, allocation and cash-versus-receivable equations remain unchanged.
