# M3-C trusted collateral attestations

`CollateralAttestationVerifier` is a Base Sepolia-only demonstration verifier implementing the existing `verify(agreementId, lotId, borrower, amount)` interface. It checks an authorized committee assertion, **not a source-chain consensus proof**. The source vault and destination manager, governance and emergency authority are immutable constructor inputs. Four distinct nonzero attestor addresses and a 3-of-4 threshold are required; test keys in Foundry are synthetic and are not operational attestor keys.

The EIP-712 domain is `name=CovenantCollateralAttestation`, `version=1`, `chainId=84532`, and `verifyingContract=<verifier address>`. The struct type is:

```text
CollateralLockAttestationV1(uint256 sourceChainId,address sourceVault,uint256 destinationChainId,address destinationVerifier,address destinationManager,uint256 sourceBlockNumber,bytes32 sourceBlockHash,bytes32 sourceTxHash,uint256 sourceLogIndex,bytes32 agreementId,bytes32 lotId,address borrower,uint256 collateralAmount,bytes32 termsHash,uint64 signerEpoch,uint64 policyVersion,uint64 expiresAt)
```

The digest is `keccak256(0x1901 || domainSeparator || keccak256(abi.encode(TYPEHASH, attestation)))`. Source chain must be 1301, destination 84532, and vault, verifier and manager must match immutable configuration. Block number, block hash and transaction hash must be nonzero. The manager must contain a Draft or Active agreement whose collateral chain, vault, lot, borrower, amount and normalized terms hash match. The manager's original deterministic agreement and lot derivation remains unchanged. Accepted attestations expire at `expiresAt` (strictly before expiry for `verify`) and must be submitted with an expiry more than now and at most 30 minutes from acceptance; signer epoch and policy version must be current.

At least three and at most four 65-byte canonical low-s ECDSA signatures must recover to unique current authorized signers; unknown, repeated or malformed signatures revert. An identical accepted digest is idempotent after authorization checks. A fresh quorum may renew the same underlying event and facts with a new expiration or signer epoch. Source event and lot bindings persist; reuse for another agreement reverts. A different quorum-signed source event/fact set for an already bound agreement sets a persistent conflict flag and emits `EvidenceConflict` without reverting; `verify` then fails. The verifier does not replace conflicting evidence and does not attempt to undo a completed draw.

Governance proposes a distinct four-signer configuration and nonzero policy version; governance can activate it only after 24 hours. Activation increments the epoch and makes prior-epoch accepted evidence fail verification until renewed. The immutable emergency authority or governance may pause immediately; only governance can unpause. While paused, new evidence is rejected and `verify` returns false, so the existing manager rejects activation and draws. Its repayment path does not call `verify` and remains available. These controls do not withdraw funds, release collateral or forgive debt. There is no unfreeze function for a conflicting agreement in this demonstration implementation.

Any two 3-of-4 quorums intersect in two signers. Conflicting approvals cannot both be produced if at most one attestor equivocates and honest signers enforce cross-epoch non-equivocation. Two unavailable signers prevent progress; three compromised signers can assert false evidence. Four keys controlled by one developer are one trust domain, not independently operated signers. The actual signer identities, custody, governance and emergency authority, source-finality observation procedure and incident handling require human approval before deployment. An authenticated Reactive callback is not independent finality evidence.

The signed schema has no `issuedAt`: the contract limits *remaining* validity at acceptance to 30 minutes, but cannot establish when an off-chain signature was created. A signer can pre-sign a future expiry, which is not acceptable on-chain until it enters the 30-minute acceptance window. Operational policy must account for pre-signed messages.
